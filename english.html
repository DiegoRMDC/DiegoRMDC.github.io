About me

I am a biologist from the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México and a science writer by vocation.

As a biologist in a family of humanists, I always thought of myself as the black sheep of the bunch. However, as soon as I started my undergraduate degree, the impulse to write about science to understand and discuss it arose in me. And so, the years taught me that talking science is as important as doing science, so I traded in my lab coat and field notes for a mouse and keyboard as I found in science communication another path.

What I do

I specialize in science communication through written media. I write journalistic articles, blogs, and technical or creative essays. I can also edit medical and technical texts.
